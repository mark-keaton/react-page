## Notes

Two Jest test files fail due to ts-jest 26.x incompatibility with TypeScript 5.x:
- `packages/editor/src/renderer/__tests__/index.test.tsx`
- `packages/editor/src/core/migrations/__tests__/migrateValue.test.ts`

Both use `jest.mock()` which triggers ts-jest's hoist transformer that relies on the removed `ts.getMutableClone` API. This will be resolved when Jest is upgraded to 29.x in Issue #13, which allows ts-jest 29.x with full TS5 support.
